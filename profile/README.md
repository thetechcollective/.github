# .the tech collective

**The tech artisans of [Implement Consulting Group](https://implementconsultinggroup.com/) — building digital solutions that are fit for humans and fit for the future.**

We are a collective of technologists, developers, testers and engineers who use technology to drive meaningful outcomes for people and the organisations they work in. We're organised as an ecosystem of small, agile teams — each a market and community leader in its own specialised domain.

Nordic roots. At home in Copenhagen and Aarhus, delivering all over the world.

🌐 [thetechcollective.eu](https://thetechcollective.eu/)

## What we do

Our teams span the technologies that carry a transformation from strategy all the way through to systems that run in production:

- **Data, AI & analytics** — data science and generative AI, from use-case discovery and rapid prototyping to models running reliably in production.
- **Quality Assurance & DevOps** — test frameworks, CI/CD, platform engineering and the developer experience that lets teams ship safely and often.
- **Conversational AI** — assistants and conversational interfaces that make systems easier for people to work with.
- **S/4HANA** — SAP-centred ERP transformation, from design through implementation.
- **Salesforce** — CRM implementation and the processes around it.
- **Cybersecurity** — securing the platforms and services our clients depend on.
- **People Tech** — the systems that support how organisations hire, develop and work.
